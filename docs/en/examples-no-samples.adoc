== Signs not covered by samples

This appendix describes further examples of traffic signs (VMS) in the static publication; no XML samples
are prepared for them. Each example contains an image marking the described parts and their indexes and a
description of the basic concept.

=== Information gantry with ZPI and lane control

image::ex-10.png[Information gantry with ZPI and lane control,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`):
** 2× variable sign on the left and right post including flashers for the whole carriageway [vms:1,5]
** 1× ZPI on the gantry for the whole carriageway [vms:2]
** 2× variable signals for lane control [vms:3,4]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
** [vms:1,3,4,5] 1× pictogram
** [vms:2] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel) and 1× text

=== Lane control gantry with static signs

image::ex-11.png[Lane control gantry with static signs,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`):
** 2× non-variable ("metal") information sign on the left and right along the road [vms:1,7]
** 3× PDZ above the lane centre lines (`laneNumber`) [vms:2,4,6]
** 2× warning or prohibitory PDZ for the whole carriageway [vms:3,5]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
** [vms:1,3,5,7] 1× pictogram
** [vms:2,4,6] 2× pictogram (main sign and secondary signal)
* Non-variable signs are described in the same way as variable ones; in the dynamic publication their
  content is always the same.

=== Gantry with a fully configurable variable sign

=== Gantry with continuous direction and traffic signs

image::ex-13.png[Gantry with continuous direction signs,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`): 2× VMS on the gantry for the whole carriageway [vms:1,2]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1,2] 1× pictogram; complex signs are described by a pictogram with
  an image and a predefined additional text.

=== Signs with traffic information along the road

* Temperature information is expressed as a text display area with 2 lines; the sign type is given by
  `vmsTypeCode`.
* Complex signs are described by the type `vms:PictogramDisplay` with an image and a predefined additional
  text.

image:ex-14.png[Temperature sign,width=300]
image:ex-15.png[Prism sign with flashers,width=270]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
** VMS (`vms:Vms`; `roadsideMounted`): 1× dynamic sign on the right for displaying temperature [vms:1]
** Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1] 1× text with 2 lines for displaying temperature
* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
** VMS (`vms:Vms`; `roadsideMounted`):
*** 1× dynamic non-continuous sign for the whole carriageway [vms:1]
*** 1× dynamic prism sign with flashers for the whole carriageway [vms:2]
** Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
*** [vms:1] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel)
*** [vms:2] 1× pictogram without further information (complex)
